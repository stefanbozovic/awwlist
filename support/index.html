How can we help?

Questions, problems, or something that just feels off? Send a support request and we’ll take a look.

Get support

For bugs, questions, or feedback, open a support request on GitHub.

Open support request

GitHub issues are public. Please do not include private or sensitive information.

Delete your data

You can remove your saved AwwList data from inside the app. Open Settings and choose Delete All Data. Individual people and lists can also be deleted from within the app.

Sign in with Apple

If you use Sign in with Apple, your Apple account is used for identity and authentication. You can also manage apps using Sign in with Apple from your Apple Account settings.
